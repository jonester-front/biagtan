Disciplines We Master

Signature 8-Limb Art

Muay Thai Legacy

The heart of our gym. Learn authentic Muay Thai — punches, kicks, elbows, knees, and devastating clinch techniques. Blends traditional Wai Kru with modern fight science. Build power, resilience, and warrior mindset.

  • ▹ Heavy bag & pad work (Thai style)
  • ▹ Clinch grappling & sweeping drills
  • ▹ Conditioning & fight cardio circuits
Beginner to advanced | Sparring optional
Pure Boxing Sweet Science

Boxing Science

Master the fundamentals of professional boxing. Footwork, head movement, combination punching, and defensive brilliance. Classes improve speed, reflexes, and ring IQ. Develop knockout precision and elite ring awareness.

  • ▹ Precision mitt work & bag drills
  • ▹ Slip rope & defensive drills
  • ▹ Technical sparring & combinations
All levels | Focus on technique
MMA Unified combat

MMA Fusion

Seamless integration of Muay Thai, wrestling, and BJJ concepts. Ideal for aspiring MMA fighters or complete 360° combat sports training. Cage drills, transitions, ground striking, and fight IQ development.

  • ▹ Takedown defense & clinch entries
  • ▹ Ground-and-pound technique
  • ▹ Fight IQ & cage management
Competition prep Advanced / Intermediate
